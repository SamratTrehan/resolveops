## Hot Take

A Verifier is not independent merely because it has a different role prompt. In CASE-003—the final known benchmark failure—the Resolver conservatively abstained and the same-model Verifier reinforced that uncertainty rather than correcting it. This observed case does not prove a universal failure mode, but it shows that role separation alone did not guarantee epistemic independence when both stages shared the same model, evidence, and uncertainty assumptions. A next experiment would test deliberately differentiated verification evidence or disagreement calibration.
